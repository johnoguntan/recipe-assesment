Jollof Rice Recipe

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 30 minutes
  • Additional Time: 15 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour
  • Servings: 6 to 8
  1. Rich and Flavorful: Jollof rice is known for its deep, smoky flavor that comes from the combination of tomatoes, peppers, and spices.
  2. Versatile: This dish can be served as a main course or as a side dish, making it suitable for various occasions.
  3. Cultural Experience: Enjoying Jollof rice allows you to experience a taste of West African culture and cuisine.
  4. Customizable: You can easily adapt the recipe by adding your favorite proteins or vegetables to suit your taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long grain parboiled rice
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot over medium heat and sauté the chopped onions.
  2. Add the red bell pepper, diced tomatoes, and tomato paste. Cook for 10 minutes.
  3. Pour in the chicken broth, thyme, curry powder, salt, and pepper. Stir well.
  4. Add the washed rice into the sauce and mix to combine.
  5. Reduce the heat, cover the pot tightly, and allow the rice to steam until cooked.
  6. Fluff the rice lightly with a fork and serve hot.

Tips

For a smokier taste, cover the pot and allow the rice to steam on low heat for a few minutes. You can also add bay leaves for extra aroma.

Chef's Notes

If rice seems to be a little undercooked at this point, stop fluffing. Pat the rice down gently with the back of the fork, cover tightly again, and set heat to medium low. Cook for another 5 minutes, and check again.
